# Contract Review

Perform preliminary risk assessment on inbound legal agreements (NDAs, MSAs, vendor contracts) to flag non-standard clauses.

## When to use

- Reviewing incoming mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).
- Spot-checking Master Services Agreements (MSAs) for unusual liability or indemnification terms.
- Summarizing key obligations and renewal dates for executed contracts.

## When not to use

- Final binding legal execution or formal legal counsel representation.
- Technical code audits or security penetration testing.

## Workflow

1. **Document Intake**: Identify agreement type and governing jurisdiction.
2. **Risk Flagging**: Scan for high-risk clauses: unlimited liability, IP assignment overreach, restrictive non-competes, or unfavorable indemnification terms.
3. **Playbook Comparison**: Compare provisions against standard company policy and acceptable fallback positions.
4. **Summary & Redlines**: Provide a bulleted risk summary and suggested redline modifications for counsel or executive review.
