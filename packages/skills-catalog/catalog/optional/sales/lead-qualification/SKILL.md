# Lead Qualification

Qualify incoming prospects systematically to focus sales efforts on high-intent accounts matching the Ideal Customer Profile (ICP).

## When to use

- Evaluating inbound demo requests or signup form submissions.
- Scoring lead intent and company size/fit before scheduling discovery calls.
- Preparing tailored discovery questions for prospect outreach.

## When not to use

- Executing direct outbound cold email campaigns without inbound signals.
- Technical architecture or engineering scoping.

## Workflow

1. **ICP Match Check**: Evaluate company industry, employee count, tech stack, and budget indicators.
2. **Intent Scoring**: Assess stated pain points, urgency cues, and engagement level.
3. **Draft Discovery Questions**: Formulate 3-4 targeted questions addressing key qualification criteria (BANT: Budget, Authority, Need, Timeline).
4. **Recommendation**: Assign tier (Tier 1: Hot/Immediate, Tier 2: Nurture, Tier 3: Disqualified) with justification.
